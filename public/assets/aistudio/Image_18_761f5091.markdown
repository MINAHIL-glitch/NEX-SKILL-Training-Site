## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a premium, future-forward technical education presence tailored for career switchers, aspiring developers, and tech professionals across emerging tech hubs. Combining high-octane cyber aesthetics with rigorous academic credibility, the interface moves past chaotic institutional layouts toward a streamlined, hyper-focused experience.

The visual style blends **Dark Mode Glassmorphism** with **Technical Precision Minimalist accents**. Deep oceanic obsidian backdrops create infinite depth, punctuated by electric cyan beam lines, glowing states, and sleek translucent surfaces. The emotional atmosphere projects mastery, industry readiness, speed, and high competence.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a rich, space-grade obsidian canvas (`#0b1120` base, sinking to `#070c18` background wells) contrasted against an electric tech spectrum:

- **Primary (`#00f0ff` Cyan Glow):** Used for focal CTAs, vital telemetry metrics, active states, and progressive completion indicators.
- **Secondary (`#3b82f6` Cobalt Tech):** Structures structural framing, interactive link transitions, focus rings, and secondary indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#ef4444` Crimson Alert):** Reserved for live batches, urgency chips, urgent admission deadlines, and critical status flags.
- **Neutral Layering:** Surface card containers utilize `#0f172a` with layered frosted glass (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)`), finished with hairline neon cyan-slate borders. Text renders in high-legibility `#f8fafc` for titles and `#94a3b8` for contextual metadata.

## Typography

The design system exclusively leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to produce a razor-sharp modern tech look. Its geometric proportions and open apertures guarantee crystal clarity even in dense curriculum outlines and code modules.

Display and hero headlines use tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor landing views with high visual impact. Meta labels, course codes, and tech stack tags leverage uppercase styling with positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to enforce an engineered dashboard feel.

## Layout & Spacing

A balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid governs desktop displays (max-width `1320px`), collapsing into 6 columns on tablet devices (`768px - 1024px`) and a 4-column stack on mobile viewports (<`768px`).

Grid margins maintain generous breathing room (`margin: 3rem` on desktop; `1.25rem` on mobile) ensuring elevated cards and glowing accents never clip viewport boundaries. Vertical cadence is dictated by an 8pt spatial baseline, using `space-md` (`1rem`) as the default gap between card content groupings and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) between distinct interactive sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on three-tier glassmorphism fused with colored perimeter diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Deep matte `#070c18` background, optionally populated by low-opacity cyan radial ambient lights (blur: `160px`, opacity: `12%`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Section Enclosures):** Dark navy glass panels (`background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)`) backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)`. Borders utilize a crisp `1px` translucent edge (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.16)`).
- **Level 2 (Hover & Floating Elements):** Lifted cards translate `Y: -4px` with a layered cyan shadow (`0 14px 34px -8px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.18)`), switching the border to full electric cyan illumination.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Highly opaque navy containers (`rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.94)`) surrounded by a dual-ring glow: `0 0 0 1px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.4)` and ambient drop shadow `0 24px 60px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp technological precision with ergonomic rounded touchpoints, adopting **Scale 2 (Rounded)**. Standard cards, inputs, and primary containers use `0.5rem` (`8px`) radiuses. 

Featured curriculum cards and hero visual containers apply `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`), while interactive badges, status capsules, and segmented pill tabs take full circular or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem`) contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Cyber:** Solid `#00f0ff` fill with jet-black `#0b1120` bold text. Subtle neon cyan glow shadow (`0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`). Active state applies a scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary Glass:** Background `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.6)` with a `1px` border of `rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.4)` and `#f8fafc` text. Hover prompts an internal blue shimmer.
- **Alert / Enroll Now:** High-energy crimson gradient (`#ef4444` to `#dc2626`) for rapid enrollment calls, flanked by a pulsating beacon dot.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with uppercase tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.08em`).
- **Live / Admissions Open:** Background `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`, text `#f87171`. Includes a pulsing red indicator dot.
- **Tech Track (AI, Full-Stack, Cloud):** Cyan translucent fill `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.1)` with crisp cyan border and `#00f0ff` text.

### Cards & Program Containers
- Frosted dark navy fill (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.72)`) with `1px` stroke `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.14)`.
- Internal structure: High-contrast header, track duration badge, mentor credentials preview, curriculum summary, and bottom-pinned "Apply for Batch" CTA.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Deep glass inputs (`background: rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.6)`) with `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)` borders.
- Focused state eliminates generic browser rings, engaging an electric cyan line (`#00f0ff`) paired with an ambient `0 0 12px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)` outward glow. Floating labels render in `#94a3b8`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom dark slate squares and circles with `1.5px` border (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.35)`). Checked state fills with solid cyan `#00f0ff` and renders an obsidian `#070c18` checkmark.

### Technical Metrics & Stat Tiles
- Compact metric blocks featuring extra-bold Plus Jakarta Sans values (`36px`) rendered in gradient text (`#00f0ff` to `#3b82f6`), accompanied by compact muted uppercase descriptions (e.g., "98% JOB PLACEMENT", "35,000+ ALUMNI").
