## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elite, forward-leaning visual language tailored for modern technical education and workforce acceleration. Combining institutional rigor with high-tech dynamism, the aesthetic projects authority, precision, and upward career trajectory.

The visual style blends **High-Precision Corporate Modern** with an **Engineered Glow Aesthetic**. Dark, deep oceanic navy canvases provide depth and focus, balanced by crisp white and slate surfaces for extended readability during intensive curriculum study.
